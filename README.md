I designed a Two Stage Operational Amplifier with gain 61dB and Phase margin around 45 degree, here i used a software called cadence virtuoso of 90nm technology
In this the two stages are:
1. Differential Amplifier
2. Common Source Amplifier
   combinedly we call it as the two stage operational amplifier

Importance of two stages is: 
a. The first stage is usually a differential amplifier that provides high input impedance and initial gain.
b. The second stage is a common-source amplifier that boosts the gain and drives the load.

A Miller compensation capacitor is connected between the two stages to ensure stability.
This creates a dominant pole and improves phase margin.
